Common Questions

I can't hear anything.

Turn up your device's media volume. Riffology plays even with the silent switch on, so check whether audio is going to Bluetooth headphones or a speaker you've forgotten about. If playback stopped after a phone call, alarm, or switching apps, tap play again.

The playhead doesn't line up with the sound on my Bluetooth headphones.

Bluetooth adds a short delay before audio reaches your ears. Open Settings → "Playhead sync" and nudge the offset until the moving playhead matches what you hear. Wired headphones and the built-in speaker usually need no offset.

Why am I seeing ads?

Ads keep the riff generator free. New players get three riffs before any ads, and after that an ad appears after every second new riff. Practicing, looping, and replaying a riff never shows an ad. If you're in the EU, UK, or Switzerland, you can change your ad consent at any time in Settings → "Ad privacy choices". On iOS you can also turn off tracking in your device's Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

How do I remove ads?

A one-time "Remove Ads" purchase is in the app under Settings → Purchases → "Remove ads". It turns off ads for good; there is no subscription.

I bought Remove Ads but I'm still seeing ads.

This can happen after reinstalling the app or moving to a new device. Open Settings → Purchases → "Restore purchases" while signed into the same App Store account you bought with. If ads still appear, email us and include roughly when you made the purchase.

I lost my saved riffs, or I'm setting up a new phone.

Riffology stores your saved riffs, practice history, and streak on your device only. There is no account or cloud sync, so they don't move to a new device and can't be recovered after the app is deleted. If you'd like sync in a future version, let us know; it helps us prioritise.

How do I keep a riff outside the app?

Tap Share on the Create screen to export the riff as an audio file and send it anywhere your share sheet can: Messages, Files, a recording app, or a bandmate.

The riffs are too easy or too hard.

Open "Shape the next riff" on the Create screen to change the level, key, and feel. To redo the whole setup, including the taste check, use Settings → "Redo setup & taste check". Your streak and practice stats are kept.

Does Riffology support other tunings or instruments?

Riffology currently generates riffs for six-string guitar in standard tuning. If you'd like drop tunings, seven-string, or bass, email us; requests shape what comes next.

The app crashed or something looks wrong.

Please email us with:

  • What you were doing when it happened
  • Your device model and iOS version (for example, "iPhone 15, iOS 26.1")
  • A screenshot or screen recording, if you have one

If a generated riff sounds wrong or its tab looks off, a screenshot of the tab helps us find it.

About the App

Riffology is a guitar practice app made by Andrew Murphy. Generate fresh riffs at your level, slow them down, loop the hard bars, and learn the notes behind them. No account required.
